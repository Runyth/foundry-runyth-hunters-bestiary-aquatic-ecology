## Carrying-capacity review

Large predators are sparse and supported by ordinary fish, seals, large migratory filterers, eels, and episodic carrion. The Trenchjaw Cetarch is represented by fewer than forty mature animals across the basin. The Coldwake Thresher follows seasonal prey fronts instead of sharing a permanent coastal apex niche. Greywake Sifters and Brineback Rakers recover slowly and receive protected months. Harbor scavengers and parasites reproduce faster but are constrained by nursery habitat, predators, oxygen, and passage.
