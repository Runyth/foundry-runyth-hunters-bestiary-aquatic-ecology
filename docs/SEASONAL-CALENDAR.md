# Imperial Seasonal Calendar

All 33 months are represented in `seasonalCalendar`. Months 28–05 emphasize cold-current movement; 06–16 emphasize flood, spawning passage, and growth; 17–27 emphasize warm low water, hypoxia risk, estuarine nesting, and harbor concentration. Each species declares exact breeding, presence, and protected months. The canonical present is `0443-03-03`.
